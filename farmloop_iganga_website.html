Who we are

A farm designed to make every resource count.

FarmLoop Iganga is an ongoing, individually led agricultural initiative by Kiyemba Isaac. The project aims to strengthen an integrated farm that combines food crops, poultry and livestock on available land in Bulowoza Village.

The long-term vision is to build a productive and resilient enterprise that contributes to local food availability, creates opportunities for work and demonstrates how crop and animal production can complement one another.

Our project priorities

Practical investment in production, farm management and responsible use of natural resources.

  • ✓Improve maize, cassava and beans production.
  • ✓Strengthen goat, pig and poultry enterprises.
  • ✓Improve animal housing, feeding and veterinary care.
  • ✓Promote soil improvement, water management and reduced farm waste.
What we do

Different farm activities. One connected system.

The project brings together food crops and animal enterprises to diversify production and create opportunities for farm resources to be reused responsibly.

🌽

Maize production

Grow maize for household food needs and potential commercial markets, with suitable crop residues considered for supplementary livestock feed.

🌱

Cassava cultivation

Develop cassava production as an additional source of food and income, with processing and marketing opportunities explored where practical.

🫘

Beans production

Produce beans to support household nutrition, income generation and diversified cropping.

🐐

Goat rearing

Expand goat production through suitable housing, feeding, breeding practices and veterinary support.

🐖

Piggery

Strengthen pig production with improved housing, hygiene, feeding, breeding and animal-health management.

🐔

Poultry keeping

Develop hen production for eggs and poultry meat through appropriate housing, feeding, clean water and biosecurity.

The FarmLoop approach

Put the farm cycle to work.

Integrated farming creates opportunities for crops and livestock to support each other. These are practices the project intends to strengthen as it develops.

01 / CROPS

Grow food crops

Produce maize, cassava and beans for food and potential sales.

02 / LIVESTOCK

Raise animals

Develop poultry, goat and pig enterprises with appropriate care.

03 / REUSE

Reuse farm resources

Use properly composted manure for soil improvement and suitable crop residues as supplementary feed.

04 / REINVEST

Build the next cycle

Maintain production and financial records, then reinvest part of farm income where possible.

12-month implementation

A phased approach to strengthening the farm.

Activities will be implemented progressively, with records kept on inputs, production, animal health, expenditure and sales.

Months 1–2

Prepare & procure

Assess activities, prepare land, source inputs, livestock and poultry, and improve facilities.

Months 3–4

Establish production

Plant crops, introduce additional animals where funded and establish management records.

Months 5–8

Manage & develop

Monitor crops, provide animal care, manage pests and diseases, and improve infrastructure.

Months 9–12

Harvest & evaluate

Harvest and store produce, explore markets, review performance and report on progress.
